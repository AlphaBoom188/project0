3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. We use these service providers to run the app, under their own data protection terms:

  • LY Corporation (LINE): sign-in, the bot, and notifications.
  • Supabase: database and file storage ([REGION]).
  • OpenAI: reading receipts, converting voice to text, understanding typed expenses and reminders, reading 50 ทวิ withholding certificates you scan, and suggesting categories for imported statement lines. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent this way is not used to train their models by default.
  • Google: verifying your email address (only if you connect Google), delivering CSV exports by email, and reading your calendar events (only if you connect Google Calendar).
  • Anthropic: if enabled by the operator, technical error details are sent for automated diagnosis; these are error messages with identifiers removed, not your financial records.
  • ngrok: secure tunnelling of connections to our server.
  • Market data (Yahoo Finance, CoinGecko): only ticker symbols are sent to fetch prices; no personal data.

In a LINE group, other members can see the bills, amounts, names and who has paid, as shown by the bot in that group. Members who owe you on a bill see the payment details you saved, and the person you paid sees the slip you sent them. Reminders set in a group are posted in that group and mention the people named in them. A shared-bill link can be opened by anyone you send it to; it shows that bill's title, amounts and participant names. We may also disclose data where required by Thai law.

4. Transfers outside Thailand

Some providers above process data outside Thailand (for example, in the United States or Singapore). We use providers that apply appropriate safeguards as required by the PDPA.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account data is kept until you delete it. Settings → Delete My Account permanently erases your transactions, categories, accounts, budgets, investments, shared bills, group memberships, payment details, payment slips, reminders, notes, tax information, tax documents and receipt thumbnails, and disconnects Google Calendar (we revoke our access with Google). Group bills you paid for, and group reminders you set, are removed from those groups.
  • Disconnecting Google Calendar in Settings revokes our access and deletes the stored token straight away.
  • A payment slip is deleted when the person you paid rejects it or cancels the bill; otherwise it is kept with the bill until either of you deletes your account.
  • Error reports are kept for up to 90 days, alert history for 180 days, scheduled-job records for 30 days, and server logs for up to 30 days.
  • Database backups, if enabled, are kept for up to [7] days and then overwritten.
  • Service providers may keep data for short periods under their own policies (for example, for abuse monitoring).

6. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Every request for your data must carry your verified LINE sign-in. Receipt thumbnails, payment slips and tax documents are stored privately and shown through short-lived links. The Google Calendar token is stored encrypted. We limit how often costly features can be used to prevent abuse. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the PDPA requires.

7. Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask to access or get a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing before withdrawal). You can export your transactions as CSV in the app and delete your account in Settings. For anything else, email [CONTACT EMAIL]; we will reply within 30 days. You may also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

8. Children

The app is not intended for children. If you are under 20, please use it only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or through the LINE bot.
